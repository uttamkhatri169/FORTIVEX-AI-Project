Lumen CRM is a lightweight CRM for small and mid-size sales teams. It tracks leads, deals and follow-ups, and syncs with Gmail and Google Calendar.

Pricing: Starter is Rs 799 per user per month (up to 3 users). Growth is Rs 1,799 per user per month with automation and reports. Enterprise is custom priced with SSO and a dedicated manager. Annual billing gives 2 months free.

Setup takes under one day. We import contacts from CSV or Excel for free. Onboarding calls are included in Growth and Enterprise.

Security: data is encrypted at rest and in transit, hosted in India (Mumbai region), and backed up daily.

Support: chat and email on Starter; phone support 9am-7pm IST on Growth; 24x7 on Enterprise. A 14-day free trial needs no credit card.

Integrations: Gmail, Outlook, Google Calendar, WhatsApp Business, Zapier and a REST API.
